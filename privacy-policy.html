Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cineinsta ("Cineinsta", "we", "us" or "our") may collect, use and protect information when you visit cineinsta.com.

Contact information

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request and to keep appropriate records of editorial, correction or rights-related matters.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations or protect the website.
