About Cineinsta

Independent coverage of Indian cinema, with a focus on useful, readable information for movie audiences.

What is Cineinsta?

Cineinsta is an independent entertainment website covering Indian cinema across Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi films, along with OTT releases and related entertainment stories.

Our aim is to make cinema information easier to discover in one place, including original cinema news articles, movie reviews, trailers, Cineinsta Buzz, OTT information and selected features.

Cineinsta Buzz brings together cinema signals and information available to Cineinsta, including OTT release information, theatre trends, review activity and other publicly available indicators where appropriate. We do not claim access to private platform rankings, ticket sales or internal OTT performance data.

Our editorial approach

Cineinsta publishes original editorial articles written for Cineinsta readers. Our editorial process may use publicly available information and multiple sources as research inputs, but we do not reproduce full articles from other publications or present another publisher's article as our own.

Our aim is to select relevant cinema developments, verify available facts, rewrite and structure the information independently, and present it in a clear format for Cineinsta readers.

Reviews and ratings

Cineinsta reviews and ratings represent Cineinsta's own editorial opinions when explicitly identified as such. Third-party ratings or scores, when displayed, belong to their respective platforms and are not owned or endorsed by Cineinsta.

Independence

Cineinsta is an independent website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, BookMyShow, YouTube, Google or any film studio, streaming service, actor, filmmaker or other third party unless expressly stated.

Corrections and feedback

If you find an inaccurate statement, outdated information, broken link or copyright concern, please contact us. We welcome factual corrections and will review legitimate requests.
