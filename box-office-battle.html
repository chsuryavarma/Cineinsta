Telugu Cinema Box Office: 2015–Present

Box Office Battle is Cineinsta’s archive of significant Telugu films from 2015 onward. It brings published trade and box-office figures into one place so readers can compare reported collections across different films.

About the Box Office Data

Cineinsta uses publicly reported trade and box-office figures. Historical figures can differ between sources because collection reporting methods and definitions are not always identical. Where available, Cineinsta retains source attribution instead of silently combining conflicting figures.

Reported footfalls are displayed only when a source explicitly reports them. Cineinsta does not estimate footfalls from collection figures. Budget, net collections, gross collections and worldwide figures are presented as reported metrics and should not be interpreted as profit.
